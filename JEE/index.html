JEE 2026 — Overview & Key Facts

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is India's premier national-level engineering entrance exam, conducted in two stages. JEE Main is the gateway to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, while JEE Advanced is the sole path to admission in all 23 IITs. Understanding the full picture before you start preparing is critical.

🏫 JEE Main

  • ✅ Conducted by NTA (National Testing Agency)
  • ✅ Two sessions: January & April
  • ✅ Best of two session scores considered
  • ✅ Admission to NITs, IIITs, GFTIs
  • ✅ Qualifying exam for JEE Advanced
  • ✅ No attempt limit prescribed by NTA
  • ✅ Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode

🎓 JEE Advanced

  • ✅ Conducted by IIT Roorkee (for 2026)
  • ✅ Held on May 17, 2026
  • ✅ Two mandatory papers (Paper 1 & Paper 2) same day
  • ✅ Admission to 23 IITs & IISc
  • ✅ Only top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers eligible
  • ✅ Maximum 2 attempts in consecutive years
  • ✅ One of the toughest exams globally

💡 Key Insight

JEE Advanced syllabus is a superset of JEE Main. It covers all JEE Main topics but in much greater depth, plus several exclusive advanced topics not in JEE Main. Preparing for JEE Advanced automatically prepares you for JEE Main, but not vice versa.

JEE Advanced 2026 — Complete Details

JEE Advanced is widely regarded as one of the toughest engineering entrance exams globally. It was conducted on May 17, 2026 by IIT Roorkee. The exam pattern changes every year, but the syllabus remains consistent.

3.1 Exam Pattern

Parameter Paper 1 Paper 2
Duration3 Hours3 Hours
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry, MathsPhysics, Chemistry, Maths
Question TypesMCQ (single/multi), Integer type, Match listMCQ (single/multi), Integer type, Paragraph
Negative MarkingComplex partial/negative schemeComplex partial/negative scheme
Max Marks~180 (varies yearly)~180 (varies yearly)
Both PapersMandatory — both on same day

⚠️ Critical Warning

The JEE Advanced exam pattern changes every year and is NOT disclosed before the exam. Multi-correct MCQs have partial marking but also full negative marking. Never assume the same pattern as the previous year. Always read all question instructions carefully at the start of the exam.

3.2 What Makes JEE Advanced Harder?

📊 Question Type Complexity

  • • Multi-correct MCQ — can have 1–4 correct options
  • • Matrix Match — complex column matching
  • • Paragraph-based — read a passage, answer 2–3 Qs
  • • Integer type — no options, pure calculation
  • • Linked comprehension — interconnected problems

🧠 Conceptual Depth Required

  • • Multi-concept problems spanning 3+ chapters
  • • Novel problem setups not seen in textbooks
  • • Deep calculus integration in Physics
  • • Proof-based Math questions
  • • Experimental & error analysis questions

Eligibility Criteria

✅ JEE Main 2026 Eligibility

🎓
Education: Must have passed Class 12 (or equivalent) from a recognized Board (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, etc.)
📅
Year of Passing: Appeared/passed Class 12 in 2024, 2025, or appearing in 2026
🔢
Attempts: No prescribed attempt limit by NTA
🎂
Age Limit: No minimum age limit prescribed by NTA
📋
Subjects: Must have taken at least 5 subjects in Class 12
🌍
Nationality: Indian nationals, PIO/OCI cardholders, and foreign nationals are eligible

For NIT/IIIT/GFTI Admission:

Minimum 75% marks in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST) OR be in top 20 percentile of your board

🏆 JEE Advanced 2026 Eligibility

1️⃣
JEE Main Rank: Must be among top 2,50,000 candidates (across all categories) in JEE Main 2026
2️⃣
Class 12 Appearance: Must have appeared for Class 12 for the first time in 2025 or 2026. Students who first appeared in 2024 or earlier are NOT eligible.
3️⃣
Attempts: Maximum 2 attempts in 2 consecutive years
4️⃣
IIT Admission History: Must NOT have been previously admitted to any IIT (including cancelled admissions)
5️⃣
Subjects in Class 12: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are compulsory

For IIT Admission (after qualifying):

Minimum 75% in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD) OR top 20 percentile of respective board

❌ Common Eligibility Mistakes to Avoid

  • Students who passed Class 12 for the first time in 2023 or earlier are NOT eligible for JEE Advanced 2026
  • Having a seat in any IIT (even if later cancelled/withdrawn) makes you ineligible for JEE Advanced forever
  • The 75% marks requirement is for IIT admission, NOT for appearing in JEE Advanced
  • Foreign nationals are exempt from qualifying JEE Main and can apply directly under supernumerary quota

Preparation Strategy & Study Plan

9.1 Month-by-Month Roadmap (12-Month Plan)

1
Months 1–2 Foundation Phase

Build Class 11 fundamentals: Mechanics, Algebra, Mole Concept. Read NCERT thoroughly. Don't jump to JEE-level problems yet. Focus on concept clarity over speed.

2
Months 3–4 Class 11 Completion

Complete remaining Class 11 topics: Thermodynamics, SHM, Waves, Organic Chemistry basics, Trigonometry, and Coordinate Geometry. Start solving module exercises.

3
Months 5–7 Class 12 Phase

Cover all Class 12 topics: Electrostatics, EMI, Optics, Calculus, Organic reactions, Electrochemistry. Start solving PYQs chapter-wise after each topic.

4
Months 8–9 Intensive Practice

Full syllabus complete. Start full-length mock tests (3–4 per week). Analyze every mock — understand every mistake. Revise weak chapters immediately. Target 200+ in mocks.

5
Months 10–11 Revision & Consolidation

Revise all formula sheets and short notes. Solve PYQs of last 10 years (JEE Main + Advanced). Focus on your weakest subjects. Improve speed and accuracy simultaneously.

6
Month 12 🎯 Final Sprint

Light revision only. No new topics. One mock per day. Sleep 7–8 hours. Review formula sheets morning and evening. Stay confident and positive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between JEE Main and JEE Advanced? +
JEE Main is conducted by NTA twice a year and provides admission to NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs (900+ colleges). JEE Advanced is conducted by rotating IITs and is the sole gateway to all 23 IITs. Only the top 2,50,000 JEE Main qualifiers are eligible for Advanced. The Advanced syllabus goes much deeper and includes question types not seen in JEE Main.
Is NCERT enough for JEE Main 2026? +
NCERT is essential but not fully sufficient. For Chemistry, NCERT covers almost everything needed for Inorganic and a large portion of Physical Chemistry. For Physics and Mathematics, you need additional problem-solving books like DC Pandey, HC Verma, and Amit Agarwal to develop the application-level skills needed for JEE Main.
How many times can I attempt JEE Main 2026? +
NTA has no prescribed attempt limit for JEE Main. You can appear in both sessions (January and April) in the same year, and the best of the two session scores is considered for ranking. Candidates who passed Class 12 in 2024, 2025, or are appearing in 2026 are eligible.
What is the NTA percentile score? Is it the same as percentage? +
No, they are completely different. The NTA percentile score reflects your relative performance compared to all other candidates — it shows what percentage of students scored below you. Even with lower raw marks, you can get a high percentile if the exam is tough. The best of two session percentile scores determines your final rank.
Can a 2024 Class 12 passout appear for JEE Advanced 2026? +
No. Only candidates who appeared for their Class 12 examination for the first time in 2025 or 2026 are eligible for JEE Advanced 2026. Students who first appeared in 2024 or earlier are NOT eligible, regardless of their JEE Main rank.
What are the JEE Main 2026 exam dates? +
JEE Main 2026 was conducted in two sessions: Session 1 from January 21–29, 2026 (Paper 1) and Session 2 from April 2–8, 2026. Session 2 results were released on April 20, 2026. JoSAA counselling began on June 2, 2026.
Should I attempt all 10 NVQs in Section B? +
Yes, absolutely. Section B (Numerical Value Questions) has NO negative marking. You attempt 10 NVQs but only your best 5 are counted. This means attempting all 10 can only increase your score — there is literally no downside to attempting all of them.
What score do I need to get into NIT Trichy/Warangal CSE? +
Top NITs CSE branches typically require ranks between 1,000–5,000 All India Rank depending on category (General). This typically corresponds to 99+ percentile in JEE Main. The exact cutoff varies each year and is released by JoSAA during counselling rounds. Always check JoSAA official closing ranks for accurate data.
