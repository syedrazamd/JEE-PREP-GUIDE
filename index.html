> FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is JEE Prep Guide completely free?

Yes, JEE Prep Guide is 100% free forever. All study material — detailed notes, short notes, formula sheets, and chapter-wise PYQs — is available without any cost, login, or subscription.

What types of notes does JEE Prep Guide offer per chapter?

Every chapter has four page types: detailed chapter notes for full theory, short notes for 15–20 minute revision, a formula sheet listing all formulas with when-to-use notes, and chapter-wise PYQs with year and exam tags.

What subjects are covered?

We cover all three JEE subjects: Physics (24+ chapters), Chemistry (Physical, Organic, Inorganic — 30 chapters target), and Mathematics (Algebra, Calculus, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors & 3D — 32 chapters target).

Are previous year JEE questions included?

Yes. Each chapter has a dedicated PYQ page at /jee/{subject}/{chapter}/pyqs with questions tagged by year and paper (JEE Main / JEE Advanced), a click-to-reveal answer, and a brief solution approach.

Is there a study roadmap?

Yes, the JEE study roadmap page gives a month-by-month plan segmented for Class 11, Class 12, and droppers, with chapter order, estimated time per chapter, and direct links to chapter notes.

Which chapters are highest weightage in JEE Main 2026?

In Physics, the highest-weightage chapters are Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, and Magnetic Effect of Current — each appearing in almost every JEE Main paper. In Chemistry, Mole Concept, Chemical Equilibrium, Ionic Equilibrium, Coordination Compounds, and p-block Elements are consistently asked. In Mathematics, Coordinate Geometry (Circles, Parabola, Ellipse), Calculus (Definite Integration, Applications of Derivatives), and Vectors and 3D Geometry are high-priority chapters for JEE Main 2026.
