/* MISSION */

    Most free JEE resources are scattered PDFs with no structure,
    or full-chapter videos you have to scrub through to find one
    formula. JEE Prep Guide fixes that: every chapter has four
    dedicated pages at clean, predictable URLs. Students search
    for exactly what they need and land directly on it.

    Content is written from scratch, verified against NCERT and
    standard JEE references (HC Verma, RD Sharma), and reviewed
    for accuracy against the current NTA syllabus.

    Target audience: Class 11, Class 12, and dropper students
    preparing for JEE Main 2026 and JEE Advanced 2026.


/* CONTENT */

    Subjects covered:
        - Physics: 24+ chapters (Mechanics through Modern Physics)
        - Chemistry: 30 chapters (Physical, Organic, Inorganic)
        - Mathematics: 32 chapters (Algebra, Calculus, Coordinate
          Geometry, Vectors, Probability)

    Page types per chapter:
        - Detailed Notes  (/jee/{subject}/{chapter})
        - Short Notes     (/jee/{subject}/{chapter}/short-notes)
        - Formula Sheet   (/jee/{subject}/{chapter}/formula-sheet)
        - PYQs            (/jee/{subject}/{chapter}/pyqs)

    Blog: JEE strategy, exam registration guides, high-weightage
          chapter analysis, entrance exam news.
